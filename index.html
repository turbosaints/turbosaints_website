About the Car

A Formula Student race car is built from specialised subsystems, each engineered to work in harmony. Here's how ours comes together.

01
The Foundation

Chassis & Ergonomics

The skeletal frame of the car, designed to keep the driver safe while supporting all other components under extreme racing loads. Ergonomic packaging ensures optimal driver positioning for sustained performance.

  • Space frame / monocoque construction
  • Driver safety cell
  • Ergonomic cockpit packaging
02
The Handling

Suspension & Steering

Responsible for keeping the tyres firmly planted on the tarmac. This subsystem dictates how the car turns, grips, and absorbs track forces — the direct link between driver input and road response.

  • Double wishbone geometry
  • Ackermann steering
  • Roll centre optimisation
03
The Heartbeat

Powertrain & Drivetrain

Manages the engine, transmission, and power delivery system — converting raw fuel into forward momentum. Every component in this chain is tuned for maximum efficiency and response.

  • Engine & intake systems
  • Transmission & differential
  • Gear ratio optimisation
04
The Control

Brakes & Wheel Assembly

Engineers the unsprung mass of the vehicle, focusing on the critical systems that stop the car safely and efficiently. Brake balance, rotor design, and bearing selection directly determine stopping performance.

  • Front & rear brake circuits
  • Rotor & caliper selection
  • Wheel bearing & hub assembly
